Key Facts

  • Earth's axial tilt: 23.5° from orbital plane
  • Summer solstice (NH): ~June 21 — longest day
  • Winter solstice (NH): ~Dec 21 — shortest day
  • Equinoxes (Mar/Sep): equal day and night worldwide
  • Seasons are caused by axial tilt, not orbital distance
